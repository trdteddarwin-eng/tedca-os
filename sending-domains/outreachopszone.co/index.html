What we do

Three ways we build your outbound motion

Every engagement is hands on. We do not hand you a slide deck and disappear. We build the system, get it producing, and document it so your team can keep it running.

Outbound Strategy and Build

We define your ideal customer, build accurate target lists, and write messaging that earns replies. Then we stand up the full sending infrastructure end to end.

  • ICP definition and segment mapping
  • List sourcing and data enrichment
  • Multichannel sequences across email and LinkedIn

Managed Sending and Deliverability

We run the day to day so your reps stay focused on calls. Inbox health, warmup, and quality control are handled by us, with replies routed straight to your team.

  • Domain and inbox warmup management
  • Deliverability monitoring and spam testing
  • Reply handling and meeting handoff

Sales Ops and Reporting

We connect outbound to your CRM so nothing slips and every meeting is tracked. You get clean data, clear attribution, and a weekly view of what is actually working.

  • CRM setup, cleanup, and pipeline stages
  • Lead routing and follow up automation
  • Weekly reporting and channel attribution
About us

A small, senior team that treats your pipeline like its own.

Outreach Ops Zone was founded by operators who spent years inside revenue teams, watching good products stall because outbound was inconsistent and nobody owned it. We started this practice to fix that for a handful of companies at a time.

We deliberately keep our roster small. You work directly with the people doing the work, not a rotating cast of account managers. That means faster decisions, sharper messaging, and a system that fits how your team actually sells.

Whether you are launching outbound for the first time or rebuilding a program that stopped producing, we bring the structure, tooling, and steady hands to get it running and keep it that way.
