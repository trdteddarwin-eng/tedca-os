What we do

Three things, done deliberately.

We do not run spray-and-pray volume. We build a small number of campaigns that say something worth answering, then refine them with your reply data.

Messaging strategy

We find the one angle your prospect cares about and build the sequence around it, so every touch has a reason to exist.

  • Audience and segment mapping
  • Core angle and offer framing
  • Multi-step sequence architecture

Design and assets

Landing pages, one-pagers, and signatures that look credible the second they land, built to support the ask instead of distracting from it.

  • Campaign landing pages
  • Branded one-pagers and decks
  • Email signatures and visuals

Copy that converts

Plain, human copy for every step, written to sound like you and tested against real replies until the open-to-answer rate moves.

  • Cold email and follow-up copy
  • LinkedIn and DM scripts
  • Subject line and CTA testing
About the studio

A small studio that treats outreach like a craft.

Outreach Art started because most cold outbound looks the same: templated, pushy, and easy to ignore. We thought a campaign should feel made, not mass produced.

We work with founders, agencies, and B2B sales teams who would rather send fewer, sharper messages than blast a list and hope. Every engagement runs through the same loop: research the audience, write the angle, design the assets, send, then read the replies and tighten what is working.

You stay close to the work the whole way. No black box, no jargon, just campaigns you would be glad to receive yourself.
