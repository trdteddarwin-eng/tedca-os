What we do

Three ways we take work off your team's plate

We pick one painful workflow, ship a tool that fixes it, then build out from there. No giant platforms you never finish rolling out.

Internal tools & dashboards

Custom admin panels, ops dashboards, and back-office apps built around how your team really works, not a template you have to bend yourself to fit.

Workflow automation

About ForgeStack

A small studio that ships, not a big agency that bills.

ForgeStack started in 2021 as two engineers who kept getting the same request from growing companies: "we have the data, we just need something simple that ties it all together." We have stayed deliberately small so the people scoping your project are the same people writing the code.

We work in tight, fixed-scope sprints, share progress every few days, and hand over clean, documented code that your own team can maintain. No lock-in, no mystery, no platform you are renting forever. When a quick automation is the right answer, we will tell you, even if it is smaller than what you came in asking for.
