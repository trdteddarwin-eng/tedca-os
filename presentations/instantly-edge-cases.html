Volume · Rotation

Scale by inboxes, never by volume

Instantly

  • •Hard cap ~30 emails/day per inbox
  • •Pools segmented by domain, provider, age, health
  • •Throttles on live reputation feedback (Postmaster / SNDS)
  • •Underperforming inboxes pulled from rotation

Tedca OS

  • ✓Caps at 20/inbox — even safer
  • ✓Rotates 12 inboxes, picks the least-used each send
  • ▲No reputation-feedback pool management yet
  • ✓send_transport gate keeps it to the 12 Zapmail boxes
Pacing · Warmup

Look human, ramp slowly

Instantly

  • •Randomized throttling, weekday-only option
  • •Gradual ramp (2 → 4 → 6 / day) on new boxes
  • •14–30 day warmup via a 4.2M-account network
  • •Read-emulation makes warmup look real

Tedca OS

  • ✓80–180s random jitter between every send
  • ✓Mon–Fri, 8am–5pm ET only
  • ✓Warmup on; pre-warmed boxes; 12→50 ramp for new ones
  • ✓Per-inbox cadence ~one email / 20–40 min
Bounces · Verification

Protect the domain from bad addresses

Instantly

  • •Keep bounces under 1%
  • •Auto-pause the whole inbox at ~3% bounce
  • •Verify before send; flag catch-all / role / risky
  • •Re-verify the list, then resume lower

Tedca OS

  • ✓Bounce → suppress the address + stop its sequence
  • ▲No per-inbox bounce-rate auto-pause
  • ✓AnyMailFinder verifies; only "valid" gets sent
  • ▲No explicit catch-all / role-based tier
Replies · Out-of-office

Read the room, don't burn the lead

Instantly

  • •Stops the sequence the moment they reply
  • •AI labels: interested / not interested / objection / referral
  • •OOO → pause until their return date, then resume
  • •Unibox: one place for every reply

Tedca OS

  • ✓Concierge stops follow-ups on reply
  • ✓Classifies interested / neutral / negative / unsub
  • ▲Detects OOO but only ignores it — no resume-on-date
  • ✓Auto-reply is price-screened; risky drafts escalate to you
Duplicates · Suppression

Never the same person twice

Instantly

  • •Auto-dedup on import (unique only, can't disable)
  • •Won't email a lead already in the workspace
  • •Global blocklist — emails and domains
  • •AI auto-blocklist triggers on reply content

Tedca OS

  • ✓Ingest dedup by real domain / normalized name
  • ✓"Ever-contacted" email guard — built today
  • ✓Suppression list + unsubscribe → do-not-contact
  • ▲No domain-level blocklist / AI auto-triggers

What to recreate

Four upgrades to close the gap

1
OOO pause-until-return-date High · Easy
Parse "back on July 5" and schedule the follow-up for after that date, instead of just ignoring the auto-reply.
2
Per-inbox bounce-rate auto-pause High · Easy
Track each inbox's bounce %, auto-pause any box over ~3% until the list is re-verified. Protects domain health automatically.
3
Domain blocklist + AI auto-triggers Medium
Block whole domains, and auto-suppress on "remove me / not interested" variants — not just an exact "unsubscribe".
4
Catch-all / role-based flagging Medium
Tier info@ / catch-all domains as "risky" and throttle or skip them.
The end-game

The one thing that's genuinely hard

Live reputation-feedback pool management. Instantly pauses and re-ranks inboxes using real-time data from Google Postmaster Tools and Microsoft SNDS.

It needs Postmaster integration and per-domain reputation tracking — a real project, but it's the highest-end deliverability move in the category. Everything else on the list gets you to ~95% of Instantly first.
